A realistic schedule for the complete platform is 6–10 months, depending on the final scope.

For a UK client, presenting the project in phases is often the most successful approach:

Phase 1: Public website + Property Management + Lead CRM
Phase 2: Customer Portal + Construction Management
Phase 3: HR + Payroll + Finance + Procurement
Phase 4: AI features, marketing automation, influencer management, advanced analytics

This phased approach reduces initial investment, delivers value sooner, and makes budgeting and project governance much easier.
